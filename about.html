About

Who's behind the investigations, and what this site is trying to do.

Hi, I'm Angad.

When I was about 15 years old, I knew that I wanted to do something in cybersecurity. Over the years, I tried a few things here and there (GRC, Bug Bounty etc.) to find what I really connected with.It turned out to be DFIR. I fell in love with solving and reconstructing cases.
Around that time, I completed my undergrad. So, I got a job! YAY! How exciting!!! "Into the real world where I'll be solving cases", or so I thought. Little did I know. Working in an organization for the first time in my life really showed me how much there is to learn, and I decided it was time to upskill. The best way to learn is by doing, so the obvious next step for me was to create an account on TryHackMe and start solving rooms. That's when my journey into DFIR really began.

I started this site because most public DFIR writeups never really helped me learn. They would just give the answer to the question without any explanation or methodology. I wanted a writeup that showed the dead ends, how one artifact correlates with another, and the reasoning that connects them, not just the final answer.

Focus areas

For now, mainly TryHackMe rooms and a few CTF's here and there. Windows forensics, Linux forensics, macOS forensics, memory forensics and mobile forensics.
